Resetting the Gut’s Metabolic Memory

Gut-wall FcRn-IgG modulation for post-diet metabolic inertia

BIO 2026 Partnering | Non-confidential overview | National Health Research Institutes, Taiwan
This page contains only non-confidential information. Detailed data are available under mutual NDA.

The Problem

Diet change can remove the trigger but leave metabolic inertia behind, allowing lipid deterioration to emerge after dietary stress is withdrawn.

The Mechanism

We identified a druggable gut-wall FcRn-IgG axis that appears to keep adverse metabolic states anchored.

The Opportunity

Axis disruption prevented delayed TG and TG/HDL elevation in vivo, supporting a source-directed approach to lipid rebound and obesity-related metabolic disease.
